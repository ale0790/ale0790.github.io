About me

Software engineer, technical leader and product co-founder.

I'm a software engineer and technical leader with 10+ years of experience designing, building and operating backend systems — including 4+ years leading engineering teams as CTO / Tech Lead.

I'm the co-founder and architect of Rang Saúde, a healthcare management platform used across multiple modules of Brazil's public health system (SUS), with integrations into several national government systems — Hórus, RNDS, SIGTAP and e-SUS AB.

I work hands-on with production performance engineering (JVM diagnostics, database query optimization) and embed generative AI / LLM tooling (Claude Code, OpenCode and multiple underlying models) across the full development lifecycle — requirements, architecture, coding, testing, code review and documentation — to raise team throughput and code quality.

Alongside my main role, I'm co-founder and technical owner of two SaaS products: Torneyo (sports tournament management) and Panel4You (queue management and service displays), owning architecture, backend, frontend and infrastructure end to end.
